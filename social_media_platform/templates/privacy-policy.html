Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026
Last Updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SocialFlow ("SocialFlow," "we," "us," or "our"), a product of GeekTechies, collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and services at socialflow.geektechies.com (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account or connect your Facebook account, we may collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your public profile information (profile picture, public profile fields)

1.2 Information from Facebook Login

If you choose to log in or connect your account using Facebook Login, we may request access to the following, depending on the features you use:

  • Basic Profile Information: name, email address, profile picture
  • Facebook Pages: list of Pages you manage, Page content, and the ability to publish posts on your behalf, if you grant Page management permissions
  • Instagram Accounts: connected Instagram business/creator account information and content, if you link an Instagram account
  • Insights and Analytics Data: engagement metrics, reach, and other performance data for Pages or ad accounts you connect, if you grant access

We only request the specific permissions needed for the features you actively use, and only with your explicit consent through Facebook's permission dialog.

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • IP address, browser type, and device information
  • Log data and usage patterns
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service
  • Authenticate your identity and manage your account
  • Enable you to schedule, publish, and manage content on connected Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts
  • Display analytics and performance data for your connected accounts
  • Communicate with you about your account or the Service
  • Improve and develop new features
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. How We Share Your Information

We may share information:

  • With Meta/Facebook, as required for the Facebook Login and Graph API integrations to function
  • With service providers who help us operate the Service (e.g., hosting, analytics), under confidentiality obligations
  • For legal reasons, if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

We do not share your Facebook or Instagram data with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 7).

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
  • Revoke SocialFlow's access to your Facebook or Instagram account at any time via your Facebook Settings > Apps and Websites

7. Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data by:

  • Removing SocialFlow from your Facebook app settings, or
  • Emailing us at development.geektech@gmail.com with your account details and a deletion request

We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
